## I build the systems behind better supply chain decisions

I am a supply chain planner and optimization builder working where operational judgment meets decision intelligence. I turn constraints across demand, inventory, capacity, sourcing, freight, tariffs, and warehousing into models and agentic tools that planners can actually use.

My experience spans food and CPG, freight analytics, and connected hardware. Across those environments, I have delivered or identified **$72M+ in impact** while staying close to the planning work itself: understanding the exception, testing the trade-off, and making the decision usable in the real world.

| PLAN | OPTIMIZE | BUILD |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Supply, inventory, capacity, and scenario response | Network design, MILP, forecasting, and total delivered cost | Planner agents, data pipelines, dashboards, and decision tools |

| **20 hours/week** returned to planners | Built agentic portfolio skills that turn fragmented operational data into decision-ready answers |

## How I think about agentic AI

The most useful supply chain agent is not a chatbot floating above the operation. It understands the planning grain, knows which constraints are real, shows its assumptions, and leaves the planner with a decision they can defend.

That is the layer I like building: **signals -> scenarios -> trade-offs -> action**.
